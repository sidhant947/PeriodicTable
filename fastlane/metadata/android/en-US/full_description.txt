This app focuses purely on providing fast and easy access to element's data.

Key Feature:

- Cleany Layout: A beautiful, color-coded periodic table optimized for effortless browsing.

- Esential Properties: Tap any element to view its name, symbol, atomic number, atomic weight, group, period of discovery, and category..

- Offline Support: Works fully offline so you can access element information anywhere, anytime, without an internet connection..

Whether you are a student looking for a quick homework references or a science lover who wants the elements in your pocket, this app keeps chemistry simple. Download it today for a streamlined periodic table experience!
